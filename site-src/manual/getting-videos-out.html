• Drag a video card to the desktop, a Finder folder or another app to get a copy. The library's own file never moves.
  • Share (right-click, the player's top bar, or full screen) opens the macOS share menu: AirDrop, Messages, Mail, Notes and any share extensions you have.
  • Save to Downloads (right-click) copies the video into ~/Downloads and shows it in Finder. It never overwrites a file that's already there.
  • To download straight to Downloads without adding to a library, choose Downloads Folder in Add Video's "Save to" menu.
