Click + in the toolbar to open Add Video. Paste a link from YouTube, Nebula or any of the 1,000+ sites yt-dlp supports.

Download
Video (default) or Audio (MP3). Audio saves just the sound as an MP3, with the title, channel and thumbnail built into the file.
Save to
Which library to add it to. Downloads Folder saves just the file to ~/Downloads as "Title.mp4" (or "Title.mp3") without adding it to a library.
Add to (MP3 only)
Videos adds the MP3 to the library like any video (it plays without a picture and shows an MP3 badge). Music adds it as a song in the Music tab. Pick either or both. Music needs to be turned on for that library in Settings › Music; see Music.
Resolution
(Video only.) The highest resolution to download: Best Available, 4K, 1440p, 1080p (default), 720p or 480p.
Quality
Balanced (default), Best (allows 60 fps) or Smaller File. The sheet shows what each means.
YouTube sign-in
Shows whether a sign-in is saved; Change… opens the sign-in settings.

For a playlist link, you can choose to download the whole playlist or just the one video. If a playlist link is cut off (its playlist ID is too short), Add Video warns you, and if YouTube still can't find the playlist, the panel says only the one video was saved so you can copy the full link and try again.

The downloads panel

The arrow button in the toolbar shows active downloads with their progress, size and speed. The title appears as soon as yt-dlp has found the video. You can cancel any download; a cancelled download just disappears from the panel.

If a download fails, the panel explains what went wrong in plain language with a button that fixes it: sign in, update the tools, choose a different quality or reconnect a library. Details shows yt-dlp's own output.

How YouTube downloads work

  • Videos are saved as H.264 MP4 with AAC audio, so every Mac plays them.
  • YouTube downloads are tried signed out first, because YouTube sometimes gives signed-in accounts only a 360p version. Your sign-in is used only when a video needs it (age-restricted, members-only, or YouTube's "confirm you're not a bot" check).
  • If YouTube turns a signed-out download away with its bot check, the app goes straight to your sign-in for the next 30 minutes.
  • Captions are fetched with the video. If YouTube rate-limits them, the video still finishes and the captions are fetched again a few minutes later.
