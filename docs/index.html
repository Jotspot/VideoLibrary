Everything in one place

A real Mac app for the videos you love

VideoLibrary wraps the power of yt-dlp in a calm, native interface, so downloading, organizing and watching just work.

Download from almost anywhere

YouTube, Nebula, Vimeo and over a thousand more sites. Pick Best, 4K, 1440p, 1080p, 720p or 480p and watch live progress with the title shown right away.

Errors you can actually fix

No walls of terminal output. Every failure is explained in plain language with a one-click fix: sign in, update yt-dlp, or try again.

HD, guaranteed

Downloads dodge YouTube's 360p-only trap, and low-quality files get a one-click Re-download in HD that keeps your progress.

Many libraries

Keep libraries anywhere, including external drives. Disconnected drives wait patiently and come back when you plug them in.

Import your own videos

Add recordings and clips from anywhere. The file name becomes the title and an early frame the thumbnail.

Captions that keep up with the speaker

YouTube's auto-captions usually stutter and repeat. VideoLibrary shows each word as it's spoken, two steady lines at a time, in the style you choose: box, per-line boxes, outline or none, any size, font and color, with a live preview.

The player

Edge to edge, with controls that know when to leave

The video fills the window. Move the pointer and the controls float in on Liquid Glass; stop, and they disappear.

  • Resize the window to fit each video, then snap back when you leave
  • Picture in Picture and an in-app miniplayer while you browse
  • Frame-by-frame stepping, 0.5×–2× speed and ±10 s skips
  • Resume exactly where you left off
  • Full screen on demand, or every time if you prefer

Suggestions

Your YouTube feed, ready to download

Browse your real Subscriptions or Home feed using your own sign-in, with no developer account needed. Search YouTube from the toolbar and save anything in one click.

  • Hide Shorts, plus clips under a length you choose
  • Size estimates before you download
  • Optional YouTube Data API as an advanced fallback

Music, if you want it

A music player that lives right alongside your videos

Turn on Music in Settings and add folders of songs. Tags and cover art are read automatically, and every folder becomes a playlist.

  • Songs, Albums, Artists and Playlists
  • Liked Songs, Recently Played and Most Played
  • Play Next, Add to Queue and a reorderable Up Next
  • A floating glass player bar, media keys and Control Center
  • Your queue is waiting when you come back

Private by design

Your sign-ins never sit in plain text

No accounts, no servers, no tracking. VideoLibrary only talks to the sites you download from.

Encrypted at rest

Cookies and tokens are sealed with AES-GCM using a key in your macOS Keychain.

Import from any browser

Safari, Chrome, Arc, Dia, Brave, Edge, Firefox, Helium and more, by profile, one service at a time.

Redacted logs

Tokens and cookies are scrubbed before anything is written to the debug log.

Self-healing

Plaintext left behind by older versions is found, encrypted and removed on launch.

Get started

Up and running in a minute

Requires macOS 26 (Tahoe) or later on Apple silicon.

Download

Grab VideoLibrary.zip, unzip it and move the app to Applications.

Allow it to open

The app isn't notarized, so the first time open System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

Install the tools

Click Install in Settings › Tools, or run:

brew install yt-dlp ffmpeg deno

Add a video

Click + and paste a link, or pick something from Suggestions. Enjoy.
